Why Pulse exists

Engineering should feel like progress.

We started Pulse after seeing talented teams spend more time coordinating tools than solving the problems they cared about. Releases were stressful, useful signals were buried, and simple tasks became another item on a long checklist.

So we brought the essentials together: thoughtful automation, real-time visibility, and practical guardrails that work quietly in the background. Pulse helps teams move quickly while keeping the confidence and context to do their best work.

We believe the best platform is the one that gives a team its attention back—and then gets out of the way.
